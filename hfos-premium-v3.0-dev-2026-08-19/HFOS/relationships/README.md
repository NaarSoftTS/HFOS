# Relationships

_People who matter, across every workspace and season._

A person who recurs gets one canonical file: `relationships/people/<slug>.md` — who they are to you, what's live, what you've promised each other. Transient contacts stay in the owning workspace's `PEOPLE.md` instead.

**Person files are private by default** — treat them like `life/`: never part of a shared surface unless you explicitly say otherwise, section by section.

**Care ≠ pipeline.** When someone is entrusted to your care, that material never feeds sales, content, or any pipeline. No exceptions.
