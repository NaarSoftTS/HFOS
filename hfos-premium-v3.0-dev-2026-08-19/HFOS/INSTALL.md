# Install

_Welcome to your **Human Flourishing OS**. Five minutes. No accounts, no configuration screens — this is a folder, and that's the point._

## 1 · Place the folder

Put this `HFOS/` folder wherever you keep things that matter (Documents is fine). Create an empty `resources/` folder **beside** it (not inside) — bulk files and finished exports will live there so your OS's brain stays light.

```
somewhere/
├── HFOS/          ← this tree
└── resources/     ← bulk + exports (created by you, used by the OS)
```

## 2 · Open it in your AI app

**Claude Cowork / Claude Desktop:** add the `HFOS/` folder as your project folder.
**Claude Code:** open a terminal in `HFOS/` and start `claude`.
**Another harness** (Hermes, OpenWork, Codex/Cursor-class): see `system/adapters/README.md` — copy the matching template to its live name and set `system/adapters/ACTIVE` to it.

## 3 · First session

Say: **"Read START_HERE.md and let's begin."**

Your coach will introduce itself and ask a few short questions — who you are at your best, what the work is for, what success may never cost. Honest half-answers are enough; the system writes your compass from them and you correct it. **You'll also name your OS** — it's yours, it shouldn't carry ours.

By the end of the first sitting: your compass has something true in it, your first workspace exists, and the system remembered both.

## 4 · What's deliberately OFF at install

- **All schedules/automations** — turn on the morning brief only after a manual one feels good
- **All connectors** (mail, calendar, tasks) — connect them in your agent app when you're ready; `system/adapters/connections.md` is the honest register of what's reachable
- **Nothing phones home.** No telemetry, no account, no dependency on us. This system runs entirely on your machine with your AI subscription

## 5 · Back it up (two minutes, once)

Put the `HFOS/` and `resources/` folders somewhere continuously backed up — iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive, or a weekly git commit. Your system will hold things you can't reconstruct; back it up before it does.

## 6 · The two gates (say them once out loud)

1. **Nothing sends, publishes, spends, or commits in your name without you.**
2. **Your compass (`foundations/`) changes only when you say so.**

The system is built to keep you at every gate. Keeping yourself there is part of operating it.

## Trouble?

`START_HERE.md` → the tour questions. If orientation feels wrong, say so in-session and have the OS fix orientation before adding anything. Community and documentation carry everything else.
