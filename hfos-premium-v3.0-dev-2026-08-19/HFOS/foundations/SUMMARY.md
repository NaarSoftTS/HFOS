## How this room grows

Depth is **one sectioned `COMPASS.md`** (Identity · Calling · Flourishing · Filters · Direction — each section the depth behind exactly one card above), plus `voice.md` (write-deep) and `boundaries.md`. All create-on-answer; nothing scaffolds empty. When a section outgrows its ceiling or its trigger fires constantly, branch it to its own single-trigger file (mature shape: `identity.md` · `calling.md` · `filters.md` · `principles.md` · `north-star.md`) — no file loads for a trigger it doesn't serve.

**Stated once, never twice:** the season lives in § Direction (or `north-star.md`), orientation lives in `NOW.md`, the card lives here.

_Regenerate this card when a foundation file changes. Do not duplicate full essays here._
