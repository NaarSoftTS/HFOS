# Foundations elicitation (clean build)

_Use when shipping an empty compass for the person to fill — not when you already researched them. The OS must still cold-open: SUMMARY can hold prompts; do not invent biography._

**Design principle: the person tells stories; the coach does the taxonomy.** Don't make them sort themselves into boxes — invite talk, then extract.

## Session prompt (coach → person)

> I'll ask a few big questions — just talk; stories beat categories. Half-answers are fine, you can pass on anything, and I'll show you what I heard before anything is written.

1. **What's your work right now, and what season are you in?** Roles, what fills the time, what matters most at this point, what's constrained.
2. **What part of the work is uniquely yours?** What comes naturally, what people come to you for, what gives you energy — and what you're good at but should probably hand off.
3. **Who is the work for, and what should it change?** The people you serve, the problems you care about, what's better for them when the work succeeds.
4. **Where is the work headed, and what must it never cost?** What you want more of and less of, what you're building toward long-term — and the parts of a good life (relationships, health, faith, character, freedom, family…) success may never take.

Then, briefly:

5. **How should the OS sound when it writes as you?** (samples beat adjectives)
6. **What must this system never decide for you?** (their words — commitments, money, people, direction; this is the human-only line)

## Read-back (before anything is written)

Coach synthesizes one page — *"here's what I think I heard"* — the person corrects. At most 1–2 follow-ups where confidence is low. **Never** invent. Mark unknowns honestly. Never re-interrogate what's already answered.

**Extract from the stories (don't ask separately):**

- Season, roles, constraints (Q1) · wiring, energy, hand-off list (Q2) · people served + change sought (Q3) · trajectory + guardrails (Q4)
- **Flourishing, three lenses:** *fruitful* — who is fed by the results (Q3) · *whole* — what must never fracture (Q4) · *overflow* — good beyond the owner (Q3+Q4). Until answered, the engine uses the house working default — life made whole, work made fruitful, good that overflows — and says so.
- **Decision filters** (3–7, proposed from the stories, confirmed in read-back) → `COMPASS.md` § Filters
- **Contribution hypothesis** (derived, per `system/core/TRACE.md` — working target until the person corrects, never a declaration): *"You appear to do your best work when you use [gifts] to solve [problems] for [people], producing [outcomes], moving toward [direction], without sacrificing [guardrails]."* Revisit each season.

Q6 is the person's **human-only list**. Land it in `foundations/boundaries.md`; the gates read from it. The system may ask to *understand* an item on this list; it never proposes weakening one.

## Files to create only when answered

- `COMPASS.md` — one sectioned depth file: **Identity** (incl. wiring + hand-off list) · **Calling** · **Flourishing** (their whole · fruitful · overflow, in their words — `TRACE.md` and the flourishing check read from here) · **Filters** · **Direction** (trajectory; Not Doing and Open Decisions when they exist). Create only the sections answered.
- `voice.md` — write-deep only; the speak-light line lands in SUMMARY's Voice card
- `boundaries.md` — the human-only list; the gates read from it

Create-on-first-write. Empty folders are worse than absent folders. Contribution hypothesis lives as a clearly-marked *derived* card in `foundations/SUMMARY.md`.
