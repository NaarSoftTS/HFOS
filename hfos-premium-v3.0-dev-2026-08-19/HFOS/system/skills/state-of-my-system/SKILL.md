# Skill — State of My System

_A monthly (or whenever) self-review. Ten minutes, produces one page you keep — and may choose to share with the community. **Nothing leaves this system unless you decide it does**; there is no telemetry, so this page is also how the product itself learns, one voluntarily shared report at a time._

## Run it

Say: **"Run my state-of-my-system review."** The session:

1. **Measures (mechanically, no judgment):** files touched in the last 30 days by room · momentum ledger counts (open needs-me / blocked / waiting / ready-to-ship) · workspaces active vs. dormant · compass card age (when did foundations last change?) · which team lenses were actually convened.
2. **Asks you four questions (coach register, one at a time):**
   - What did the system carry this month that you used to carry in your head?
   - Where did it get in your way, feel heavy, or go quiet?
   - One win worth telling — impact or financial, small counts.
   - Scale of 1–10: how much is this system *yours* now? What would half a point higher look like?
3. **Writes the page** to `system/memory/reviews/YYYY-MM-state-of-my-system.md`: the measurements, your four answers verbatim, and one suggested adjustment (a single simplification or activation — never a list).

## The sharing choice (always yours)

The page is INTERNAL by default. If you want to share it with the community — wins and friction both make the product better for everyone — say **"make a shareable version"**: the system produces a copy with the reveal filter applied (names, clients, and numbers you haven't cleared stay out) for you to review and post yourself. Sharing is welcomed, never required, and never automatic.

## Rules for the session

- Measurements are honest counts, not scores; the system never grades the person
- An unused room or lens is information, not failure — the suggested adjustment may well be "remove/ignore this"
- Answers route nowhere else: this page is not pipeline, not marketing material, not input to anything without the person's explicit act
