# Skill — Flourishing Check (the plan gate)

_Run when shaping anything with a person on the other end: an offer, a product, a plan, a program, a commitment that touches humans. The ship gate (`reveal-check`) asks "may this leave?"; **this gate asks "should this exist?"** Ten minutes, before the work is built rather than after._

## When it runs

Before committing to any plan whose success depends on other people's behavior, money, attention, or trust — and any time the person asks "should I do this?" about something consequential. The system proposes running it; the person can decline (their call, stated).

## The procedure

**1 · Name the human on the other end.** Concretely — who receives, buys, joins, or bears this? A plan whose beneficiary can't be named isn't ready for this check.

**2 · The owner's own filters first.** Load the compass (`foundations/SUMMARY.md` — the filters and boundaries cards). Test the plan against *their* stated filters and what success may never cost. A conflict with their own boundary is a finding, not a footnote. (Before elicitation has filled the filters, say so honestly and run only steps 3–5.)

**3 · The flourishing test — three questions from the house definition:**
- **Whole:** what does this plan cost elsewhere in the owner's life or the recipient's? Who or what absorbs the strain if it works? (The most-skipped and most valuable question.)
- **Fruitful:** does it produce results that genuinely feed the people it touches — or activity, engagement, and output that merely measures well?
- **Overflowing:** who beyond the owner benefits if this succeeds? Who bears cost without voice? If all the good stops at the operator, say so.

**4 · The success case at full scale.** Most bad plans are only tested against failure. Run success: it works, it triples, everyone adopts it. **Does it still serve people well at that size, or does it only work while it's small?** A plan that must stay small to stay good is a finding.

**5 · The standing check.** Would this make the operation more impressive and any person less free — the owner, the customer, the team? That includes dependency by design, urgency manufactured to drive behavior, and anything optimizing engagement over benefit. Those fail regardless of revenue.

## Output

A short verdict in the plan's own file or session: **proceed / proceed with named changes / hold**, with each finding cited to the question that raised it. The system recommends; **the person decides** — and a decision against a finding is theirs to make, recorded without argument.

## What this skill never does

Never moralizes beyond the owner's own compass and the five checks above · never blocks — it informs the person at the gate · never treats profit as a finding (capital constrains, never sorts — a plan can be profitable and flourishing-true; the check only flags good that exists *at someone's silent expense*).
