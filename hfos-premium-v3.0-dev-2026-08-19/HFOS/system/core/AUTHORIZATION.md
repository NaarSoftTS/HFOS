# Authorization — who may know

_The companion question to `DISCLOSURE.md`. Classification decides who may **hear**; authorization decides who may **know** — which sessions, loops, and (later) people may load what. Kept as its own small page so multi-operator use extends this file instead of reworking the system._

## Today: single operator

- **The operator** has full access — every room, every class. There is nothing in this system its owner may not see.
- **Sessions** load JIT per the kernel: least-sensitive context sufficient for the task. A RESTRICTED load is an event worth being explicit about, never ambient.
- **Loops** are scoped by their charters. A loop granted no RESTRICTED retrieval simply never loads it; the briefing loop's ceiling is stated in its own charter. A loop may never widen its own scope.

## Tomorrow: shared workspaces (Team)

When a workspace is shared, this page grows a table — one row per person: which workspaces they may load, and their reveal ceiling inside each. The rules already hold by construction: `life/`, `relationships/people/`, and `foundations/` are **never** inside a shared surface; a collaborator's session loads only the shared workspace plus their own system. Nothing else needs to change shape — that is why this page exists now.
