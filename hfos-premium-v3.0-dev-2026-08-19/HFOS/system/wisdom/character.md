# Wisdom — Character (pace · integrity · tone · energy)

_JIT when: overwhelm, burnout, pace, rest, integrity, anger, message tone, saying no, habits, recovery after failure, calendar/energy, “I can’t keep this up.”_
_Path: `system/wisdom/character.md`. Always load `system/wisdom/CORE.md` first._

---

1. **Guard what you feed your mind.** Attention and looped stories become how you lead.
2. **Self-control is freedom.** Without it, inbox, urge, and the loudest voice run you.
3. **Calm, firm words beat hot ones.** Soft isn’t weak—it’s power under control. Rage spends what you’ll owe.
4. **Stay correctable.** Defend truth, not ego. Refusing to be wrong is worse than being wrong.
5. **Approval-seeking traps you.** People-pleasing and playing to the crowd capture decisions.
6. **A clear no is kinder than a slow maybe.** Delayed soft nos create false hope and clog the system.
7. **Do the work; let results speak.** Self-hype thins real honor.
8. **Simple peace beats luxurious conflict.** Drama is a tax—even when the table is rich.
9. **Protect non-negotiable rest and calendar blocks.** What you constantly steal from first (sleep, mornings, prayer, marriage time) is what the system will destroy.
10. **Recovery after failure defines you**—fall, turn, rise—not never failing.
11. **You become your circle.** Walk with the wise; distance cynicism, cruelty, and drift.
12. **Practice gratitude and steady joy.** Disciplines, not prizes for green metrics.
13. **Rest is part of the job.** Leaders who never restore eventually decide badly and call it destiny.
14. **Don’t force intimacy, loyalty, or spiritual intensity.** Real commitment has a right pace.
15. **When overloaded, cut load—don’t only add coping.** Coping on top of an impossible stack is how collapse gets scheduled.

## Extra tests

- Optimizing for approval or for truth?
- Is rest the first thing stolen under pressure?
- Same person in private and public?
- Clear no where a maybe is hanging?
- Is the fix fewer commitments, not more hacks?
