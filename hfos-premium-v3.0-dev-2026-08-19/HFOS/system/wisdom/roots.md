# Wisdom Roots — Core Principles

_Depth only. Not loaded by default. The source principles compressed into `CORE.md` + facets; ops use the plain files. Worldview-neutral by design — the owner's own convictions live in `foundations/` and outrank everything here._

---

## The core wisdom

1. **Get wise before you get ambitious.** Learn, ask, listen, think, and take correction.
2. **Do what matters, and do it well.** Skill, focus, consistency, and good judgment beat raw effort.
3. **Solve real problems.** Opportunity is usually a need you can serve better.
4. **Create more than you consume.** Build useful things, systems, relationships, skills, and assets.
5. **Build assets, not appearances.** Own things that keep producing. Don't spend to look successful.
6. **Use leverage wisely.** Good people, tools, systems, technology, and capital multiply you. Bad ones multiply problems.
7. **Choose people carefully.** Your spouse, friends, partners, employees, and advisors will shape your life more than most decisions.
8. **Character compounds.** Tell the truth. Keep your word. Be fair. Protect your reputation. Don't trade integrity for short-term gain.
9. **Control yourself before trying to lead anything else.** Manage your mouth, temper, appetites, ego, money, attention, and habits.
10. **Stay humble enough to change your mind.** Pride makes you blind. Correction makes you better.
11. **Think ahead, then act now.** Plan for tomorrow without assuming tomorrow is guaranteed.
12. **Take care of small things.** Neglect compounds just as surely as good habits do. Disorder eventually becomes expensive.
13. **Money is a tool, not the point.** Earn it, save it, invest it, use it to create freedom — and never let needing it make your decisions for you.
14. **Avoid unnecessary bondage.** Debt, dependency, addiction, bad commitments, and uncontrolled spending reduce your freedom.
15. **Be generous once you have something to give.** Wealth, knowledge, influence, and power are best used to make other people's lives better.
16. **Lead with a clear picture of where you're going.** People can endure difficulty when they understand the direction and believe the destination matters.
17. **Let results speak.** Don't spend your energy proving yourself. Become worth respecting.
18. **Expect problems. Get back up.** Failure isn't unusual. Staying down is.
19. **Build for longer than yourself.** Pass on wisdom, capability, character, relationships, and productive assets — not just money.
20. **Don't win at work and lose at life.** Relationships, health, peace, meaningful work, rest, and enjoyment are part of success — not rewards you postpone until afterward.
