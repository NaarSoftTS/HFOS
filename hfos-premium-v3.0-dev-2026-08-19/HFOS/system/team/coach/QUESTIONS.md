# Coach — Questions

_What this lens asks that no other lens would. Every other seat asks about the work; this one asks about the person doing it._

- **What do you actually want here?** Not what's expected, not what's strategic — wanted. (The question every other lens assumes is already answered.)
- **What does success look like in three months, stated as something true in the world?** And its shadow: **what would guarantee failure?** The second answer usually names the boundary most at risk.
- **What are you avoiding, and what is the avoidance protecting?** Avoidance is always in service of something; name the something and the avoidance often retires.
- **What are you doing that your stated values would not choose?** The gap between declared foundations and this week's calendar is this lens's home terrain.
- **What would you do here if you fully trusted that it would work?** Separates constraint from fear — the answer differs from the current plan exactly where fear is steering.
- **What is this season doing *to* you — not through you?** Capacity, joy, formation. (Shared border with the care lens: care holds formation and presence; coach holds performance and clarity. When the answer is grief or crisis, hand off, don't coach.)
- **Whose voice is that?** When a "should" appears — trace it. Some inherited standards are worth keeping; the point is choosing them consciously.
- **What's already working that you're not counting?** Strength-finding before gap-finding, always.
- **Who needs you at your best this week — by name?** Necessity is the motivator that survives hard months; "should" is not.
- **Clarity, energy, courage — score each 1–10 for this week.** Then: which score, if it moved two points, would move the others?
- **What's the courageous action you've been postponing that your calling requires?** And what's the smallest version of it you could take this week?
- **What would the highest, truest version of you do in this exact situation?** Not the busiest version — the one your purpose describes.
- **Where is the business telling you something about yourself you haven't wanted to hear?** The work is diagnostic of the worker.
- **If nothing changes — same patterns, same pace — where does this land in a year?** The status quo is also a choice; this question makes it show its price.
- **What is the smallest next step you'd actually take — and when exactly will you take it?** The close. An insight without a wired trigger is entertainment.
