# Make it yours

_This is not our software running on your machine. It's your system — and the whole design assumes you'll change it._

## The philosophy

You own every file. Rename rooms' contents, rewrite the docs, tune the coach, add workspaces for things we never imagined, delete what you don't use. The system's only fixed points are the ones that protect *you*: the two gates, fail-closed honesty, and the compass being yours alone. Everything else is preference, and your preferences win.

Two habits make customization compound instead of sprawl:

1. **Change the pattern where it lives.** If the OS keeps doing something you don't like, find the file that tells it to (ask it — "what makes you do X?") and change that file. One edit beats correcting it every session.
2. **Log the decision.** A one-line entry in the owning `DECISIONS.md` ("2026-09-01 — briefs now skip the calendar section; I check it myself") means the system — and every future upgrade — knows it was chosen, not accidental.

## What upgrades will and won't touch

Upgrades arrive as **packets** your own system applies (see `upgrade-guide.md`). The rule they follow: **merge, never overwrite.** Your changes are treated as decisions — a packet that conflicts with something you've customized keeps yours and flags the conflict for you to settle. You will never lose your version silently. Cancel the membership and everything installed stays yours.

## Growing it

Add a specialist lens when a real piece of work needs one (`system/team/`). Stand up a new workspace the day a new body of work is real. Turn on the morning brief when manual ones feel right. The system is built to be grown into, not configured up front — **the best version of this OS is the one that looks like you a year from now.**

## Shrinking it

Equally honored. If a room sits unused, ignore it — empty costs nothing. If structure feels heavy, say so in-session; the right move is usually less, and the OS knows how to compress. The standing check, which applies to your system too: **impressive but less free = failed.**
