# Operator's manual

_How to live in this system day to day. Ten minutes to read; the OS itself enforces most of it so you don't have to remember._

## The shape

Eight rooms, and every folder name coaches what belongs there. `foundations/` is your compass. `life/` is what's entrusted to your care — private by construction. `relationships/` is people who recur. `workspaces/` is every body of work — one business or ten. `momentum/` is the short lists that move. `system/` is how the OS thinks (you rarely touch it). `inbox/` is capture. `exports/` is the ship shelf pointer.

**Work goes in workspaces; life stays in life.** That line is also the sharing line — if you ever share a workspace with a collaborator, nothing personal can ride along, because it never lived there.

## A session

Your agent always loads three files — the kernel, `NOW.md` (the season), and your compass card — then narrows to whatever the work touches. You never paste your life story; if you feel the need to, say so — the compass card is too thin and the OS should fix it.

## Where things go (the five that matter)

- Something only *you* can do → `momentum/NEEDS_ME.md`
- A promise you made to a person → `momentum/COMMITMENTS.md`
- Stalled and someone should be chased → `momentum/BLOCKED.md`; on someone else's clock with a check date → `momentum/WAITING.md`
- Finished, awaiting your go → `momentum/READY_TO_SHIP.md`
- A permanent decision → the owning workspace's `DECISIONS.md` — one line of what and why, never pruned

Everything else: current truth lives in the subject's `STATE.md`, evidence in `TIMELINE.md`, raw drops in `inbox/`. Unsure? Ask — the OS proposes a path rather than guessing.

## Memory, honestly

The system remembers by writing things where they belong, not by keeping everything. Files have ceilings; when something grows past its ceiling the OS branches detail out and compresses — that's health, not loss. Empty is honest: an empty `NEEDS_ME.md` means nothing needs you, not that the system forgot.

## The two gates (worth repeating)

Nothing sends, publishes, spends, or commits in your name without you. Your `foundations/` change only by your say-so. Every session, every loop, every agent inherits both.

## Rhythms

Everything is on-demand at install. "Run my morning brief" gives you the day, coached. When manual briefs feel right, `system/automations/` explains turning on a schedule. Complexity is always opt-in.

This version also **adapts to your usage**: each session leaves a one-line note of what kind of work was asked (never the content), and once a month "Run my system adaptation" reads the pattern and *proposes* reshaping — moving rarely-needed material behind just-in-time pointers so the system stays as lean as possible for the load you actually carry. It proposes; you decide. Fair warning, stated plainly: this system reads and writes more than the Free version by nature — it spends tokens to buy back your attention, and heavier throughput belongs on the larger AI plans.
