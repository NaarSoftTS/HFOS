# Start here

_Welcome. This is your **Human Flourishing OS** — a human-centered, values-first agentic operating system: a structured brain any capable agent can enter so it works **with** you, in your voice, from your values. Values-led. Wisdom-guided. Context-aware. **Human-authored.**_

**AI prepares. You choose. The system remembers. The work shows.** The model is a wheel, not a pipeline — you at the hub, with values → wisdom → contextual intelligence → action → flourishing → learning turning around you; every spoke passes through you. The aim: **life made whole, work made fruitful, good that overflows** — intelligence in service of what matters.

## In one minute

1. Open this folder as the project / workdir in your agent app (Hermes, Claude Cowork, OpenWork, …).
2. Ask: **“Give me a tour — what do you know about me, and what matters now?”**
3. Then: **“What needs me this week?”**

If those two answers feel true, the OS is alive. If not, say what’s wrong — fix orientation before adding features.

## Your first session (fresh install — do this once)

Say: **“Coach, let’s begin.”** The coach lens (`system/team/coach/`) runs the short elicitation in `foundations/elicitation.md` — a few big questions about your work and its season, what's uniquely yours in it, who it's for, and what success may never cost. Half-answers are fine; the system writes your compass card from them and **you correct it** — nothing about you is ever invented.

In the same sitting: **name your OS** (it’s yours; the agent updates the kernel title), and stand up your **first workspace** (“set up a workspace for …”). That’s the whole first session: compass started, system named, one workspace live, one real item routed. Depth comes later, one card at a time.

## How the agent should load

Always: `AGENTS.md` → `NOW.md` → `foundations/SUMMARY.md` → then only the workspace / person / file the work needs.

You should **not** need to paste your life story each session. If you do, the kernel or SUMMARY is too thin.

## Your gates (non-negotiable)

- Nothing sends, publishes, spends, or commits **in your name** without you.
- Your `foundations/` (compass) change only when **you** say so.
- AI prepares options; **you** choose.

## Rooms (plain language)

| Folder | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `foundations/` | Who you are, why you work, values, voice, boundaries |
| `life/` | Health, family, home — when there’s something real to hold |
| `relationships/` | People who recur |
| `workspaces/` | Each body of work you build |
| `momentum/` | The short lists that move: needs you, commitments, blocked, waiting, ready to ship |
| `system/` | How the OS thinks and runs (you rarely edit this day to day) |
| `inbox/` | Drops that haven’t been filed yet |
| `exports/` | Pointer only — finished files live in your resources/exports store |

## First week (suggested)

1. Read `foundations/SUMMARY.md` — correct anything that isn’t you.  
2. Skim `NOW.md` — is the season right?  
3. Put real items only in `momentum/NEEDS_ME.md` (if nothing needs you, leave it empty — empty is honest).  
4. Open one workspace `HOME.md` and do one real task with the agent.  
5. Optional: turn on a morning brief **after** a manual run feels good.  
6. Specialists (`system/team/`) stay off until a piece of work truly needs one.

## The guides

Everything deeper lives in `docs/` — the operator's manual, the coach guide, security, upgrades, troubleshooting. Ask your OS or read them directly.

## Multi-platform

Host details live in `system/adapters/`. The rest of the tree is portable. If you change machines or apps, update the adapter — not your foundations.

## When something feels heavy

Say so. The right move is usually **less structure**, not more — compress, branch, or delete hollow folders. The standing check applies to your OS too: impressive and less free = failed.
