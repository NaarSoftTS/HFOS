# Life

_What's entrusted to your care — health, family, home, finances, formation. **This room is yours alone: it is never part of any shared surface,** and the OS treats it with corresponding weight._

Add a folder only when there's something real to hold (`life/health/HOME.md`, `life/home/HOME.md`, …). Same face grammar as workspaces: `HOME.md` first, depth as it's earned. Nothing here is scaffolded in advance — an empty life room is honest until life fills it.

Work things do not live here; they live in `workspaces/`. The separation is the point: it keeps work shareable and life private, and it helps you hold the two apart.
